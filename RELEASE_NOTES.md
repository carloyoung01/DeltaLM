## Contents
- History-dependent local relation substrate; bounded operator-tree recombination.
- Observation and JSON state snapshots/continuation CLI.
- Ablation, counterfactual, history-order, finite-run numerical experiments.
- Image, text, audio and video **feature adapters**, not end-to-end perception models.
- Mathematics and test limitations in Chinese and an English README.

## Evidence boundaries
No pretrained LLM weights, no general AI claim, no new mathematical primitive discovery, no energy-savings claim, and no proof of universal or infinite-time stability.
