# DeltaLM — Open Evolution Showcase · Research Preview

Author: **Carlo Young** · Source license: **Apache-2.0**

Ten falsifiable demonstrations from the unchanged DeltaLM research kernel. Scientific center: 07 (historically altered effective generative conditions); visual entry points: 05 (microcosm), 06 (no-end local world); explicitly unresolved: 04 (unknown morphology), 10 (biology-inspired toy conditions).

**What you can inspect:** real recorded relation/contact states, executable tree rewrites, three matched counterfactuals, noncommuting histories, quiet intervals and relation dissolution, screenshot-independent JSON traces, reproducible Python scripts, and an offline interactive HTML replay.

**What this does not show:** autonomous discovery of arbitrary new mathematical operators, real animal generation, biological evolution prediction, direct text/image/audio generation, or a general-purpose large language model.

**Reproduce:** `python -m unittest showcase/test_integrity.py -v`, `python showcase/run_all.py`, `python showcase/build_observatory.py`. Use `python showcase/play_world.py` for live local interaction without a win condition.

To open the release-ready notebook, start at `showcase/README.md`, `showcase/RESULTS_ZH.md` and `showcase/observatory.html`.

*Research preview: external features, readout, chosen stimuli and rendering are separated explicitly from DeltaLM's own core dynamics.*
