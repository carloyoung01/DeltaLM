Evidence, not a spectacle requirement

07 compares the same full snapshot with future rewriting permitted or frozen. The intervention is an experimenter action; it is not the kernel choosing to rewrite its own primitive grammar. Contacts can change and derived relations may later participate in new contacts, but the set of basic mathematical operators remains fixed.

High visual complexity does not prove a new effective generative law. Noncommutative histories do not alone prove emergence. We report small, null and negative results; no visual scene is silently invented to match a desired ending.

What 01–03 actually measure

Text, digit image, and synthetic sound tests compare handcrafted adapter features to DeltaLM's evolved numerical representations, each read with the same external nearest-neighbor classifier. They do not measure standalone language, image or audio generation. See results/01_03_references.json and run_all.py.

04 / 10 · The unknown stays open

A scikit-image example cat photo and a synthetic checkerboard provide numerical starting structures to 04. No "new animal" was generated. The ecology-themed schedules in 10 are synthetic: no heredity, fitness or real observed population series was implemented. Its next-contact suggestion is an external observer conditioned on current model structure, not autonomous scientific planning.
